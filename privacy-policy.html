Skyfox Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Skyfox Dash does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

  • The game does not ask for an account, name, email, or location.
  • The only data it keeps is your best score, saved locally on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • The game makes no network requests, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or third-party tracking.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released.

Questions: contact the developer at the email address shown on the game's Google Play page.
